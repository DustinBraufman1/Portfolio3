GMMB

Experience Overview

During my time as a Digital Assistant Account Executive at GMMB, a political communications agency, I supported the ideation, design, and execution of digital advertising for client campaigns. I helped concept, produce, and traffic ad creative across digital platforms, working closely with clients and in-house teams to keep materials aesthetically pleasing, on-message, and moving under tight deadlines. I created assets for social media, digital display banners, digital billboards, and high-impact homepage takeovers and newsletters (as seen at left).

Meta Advertisements

One of my main tasks at GMMB was to design and write copy for social ads running on Meta and Snap. We worked with a range of political campaign and ballot initiative clients and often had to build creative and a distinct look/feel from scratch for specific campaign messaging, rather than following existing brand guidelines. That improvisation pushed me to trust my own design instincts and taught me how to quickly build a cohesive, persuasive look from nothing to effectively communicate with voters.

To the right are three paid social graphics I designed for an independent expenditure political client highlighting Pennsylvania CD-01 nominee Bob Harvie's endorsements. These ads ran on Meta and supported a campaign that helped elect Bob Harvie by over 5 points.
